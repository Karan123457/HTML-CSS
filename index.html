Guides: Counselling & Admission

🔎 Counselling Process (General)
  1. Register on your state/board portal; verify mobile & email.
  2. Upload documents (10th/12th or ITI marks, domicile, category, photo, signature).
  3. Pay counselling fee; keep receipt.
  4. Fill choices: order by course first, then college.
  5. Lock choices before deadline; download the final list.
  6. Seat allotment: check round‑wise result & cutoff.
  7. Freeze/Float/Slide based on satisfaction.
  8. Report to institute with originals + fees (see checklist below).
🧭 Admission Steps (Institute Reporting)
  • Carry originals + 3‑5 sets of self‑attested xerox.
  • Pay admission fee via DD/UPI/online as instructed.
  • Medical fitness & character certificates (as required).
  • Hostel form (optional) + local guardian details.
  • Collect fee receipt, provisional admission letter, and timetable.
📄 Documents Checklist
  • 10th mark sheet & certificate; 12th/ITI (if applicable)
  • Domicile/caste/EWS/certificate (if claimed)
  • Transfer/Migration Certificate
  • Photo, signature, ID proof (Aadhaar)
  • Recent passport photos (8–10)
💡 Choosing Government vs Private

Government colleges: lower fees, strong peer group; Private: better infra & sometimes training. Prioritise course quality, industry tie‑ups, and placements over brand alone.
